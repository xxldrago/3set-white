<objective>
Derivation core (ANL-01/02/03): the pure math that turns raw paid orders and daily aggregates into
MRR/ARR, the MRR waterfall, and ARPU/ARPPU — computed on the fly per D-12 so formula changes never
require a re-roll of history.

Purpose: the research resolved two schema facts that would otherwise silently break these metrics —
`keyId` is NULL on new orders (join via `COALESCE(key_id, provisioned_key_id)`) and `days` is NULL on
upgrades (spread by `max(1, remainingDays/30)`). This plan implements the prescribed model and pins it
with the reconciliation identity as a test assertion.

Output: `lib/analytics/mrr.ts`, `lib/analytics/waterfall.ts`, and three Wave-0 pure test files.
