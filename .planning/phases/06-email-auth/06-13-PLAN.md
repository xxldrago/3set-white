<objective>
Close WR-02 and WR-04: registration leaks account existence via a distinguishable 409 and is unthrottled, and because email trials key on `customerRef = email:{userId}`, plus/dot aliases let one mailbox mint many accounts and trials. Add canonical-mailbox uniqueness so aliases collapse to one account, rate-limit registration on the trusted client IP, and document the residual 409 as an owner-accepted enumeration surface (D-89 precedent). Email verification stays out of scope — D-79 forbids it.

Purpose: make registration a controlled surface rather than a free oracle/account factory.
Output: schema field + migration, a canonicalization helper, throttled/normalized register route, and integration tests.
</objective>
