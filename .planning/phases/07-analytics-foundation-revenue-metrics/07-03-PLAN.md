<objective>
providerCost capture (ANL-12): fix the real per-order provider expense at the moment of key
provisioning, and surface it to admins without ever leaking it to customers.

Purpose: this is the money-path change of the phase. The capture must be (a) concurrent so key
delivery latency is unchanged, (b) non-blocking so a quote outage never breaks provisioning
(D-06), and (c) admin-only so provider economics never reach the cabinet or bot (Pitfall 5).

Output: worker + orders-service capture wiring, admin-only read path, and the provider-cost test
file (Wave-0 `analytics-provider-cost.test.ts`).
